    The Survival Codex

    The Goal

    You begin with two settlers in a single timbermellow forest. Live off the land until it runs dry, then push outwards into the wild frontier and take tiles from rival villages until you discover Farming.

    The Four Seasons

    • Spring: 4 hours per human. The year begins anew.
    • Summer: 6 hours per human. Abundant daylight for construction.
    • Autumn: 4 hours per human. The harvest doubles food gathering, and resources regrow across all held tiles.
    • Winter: 2 hours per human. No food can be gathered. Anyone without a roof dies of exposure.

    Resources & Land

    • Timbermellow: Food. Every person and soldier eats 1 per turn. Excess beyond barn capacity is lost!
    • Wood: Required for barns and technologies. Regrows in autumn.
    • Stone: Required for winter houses. Finite: stone on a tile never regrows once mined.
    • Grain: Found on plains; becomes edible food once Farming is researched.

    The Garlocks

    A ferocious faction dwells far across the map. When your food stores reach 25 or more, scouts will spot you and an attack will come. Train soldiers to defend the settlement!
